Privacy Policy

Citron for Mac · Last updated 7 November 2026
In short: Citron does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your files never leave your Mac.

Who we are

Citron is published by SARTHITECH PRIVATE LIMITED ("we"). Contact: harshaggarwal1346@gmail.com.

Your files

Every conversion, compression and edit is performed locally on your Mac. Citron does not upload, transmit or retain copies of your files. Results are saved only where you choose (next to the original, or a folder you pick).

Data we collect

None. Citron has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Citron makes no network requests of its own.

Data stored on your Mac

Citron keeps a few things locally so it works as you expect: your settings, the folders you allowed it to save to, and the number of free actions used (kept in your Mac's keychain). This information stays on your Mac and is never sent to us. Deleting Citron removes its settings.

Purchases

The optional lifetime unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Citron does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
